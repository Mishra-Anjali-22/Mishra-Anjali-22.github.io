Kickstarter Crowdfunding Analysis

What makes a crowdfunding campaign succeed? I analysed 300K+ Kickstarter projects (2009–2019) and built the same story in Excel, Power BI and Tableau using a star-schema data model.

  • 38% success rate — 51% of projects fail, ~9% are cancelled
  • Goals under $10K succeed ~46% of the time; very large goals rarely do
  • Chiptune, Residencies, Dance lead with 65%+ success rates
  • Timing matters — success peaks in October; Mon/Tue launches beat weekends
